<threat_model>
Static course-content phase. No auth, database, API, secrets, production
systems, or live enterprise integrations are introduced.

Trust boundaries:
- Public examples must be synthetic and must not include real customer,
  employee, financial, tax, legal, credential, or production-system data.
- Templates must not imply AI owns final professional judgment in high-risk
  domains.
- Course pages must stay self-contained and not require ignored
  `reference/repos/` content.

STRIDE register:
- T-01 Spoofed professional authority: templates could imply AI can replace a
  licensed or accountable professional. Mitigation: include human approval,
  source, audit, and boundary fields in templates.
- T-02 Information disclosure: examples could accidentally look like real
  customer, employee, finance, HR, legal, tax, or credential records.
  Mitigation: use obviously fictional/sample markers and run sensitive-pattern
  scans.
- T-03 Link integrity failure: overview pages could link to files that were not
  created. Mitigation: verify all overview links point to created files.
</threat_model>
