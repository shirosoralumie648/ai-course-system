## Established Patterns

- Week pages use nine Course C sections: business situation, role boundary,
  inputs, AI workflow, tool/application integration, Reference use, compliance,
  assistant-pack update, acceptance.
- Lab pages use seven sections: goal, task, input materials, steps, submission,
  acceptance, scoring.
- Sidebar links only existing files.
- VitePress build must run with `BASE=/ai-course-system/`.
